## Talk to your friends and family

The people who already trust you are the most likely to actually listen, and a handful of conversations inside one circle does more than a message broadcast to thousands. Our guide covers how to open the conversation, how to listen well, and what to suggest at the end.

<div class="btn-row"><a class="btn" href="/talk-about-ai-risk">Read the conversation guide</a></div>
